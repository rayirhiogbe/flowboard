Questions

Straight answers.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage, on this device. Clearing site data removes it, so export a JSON backup if the work matters.

Can I use it on more than one device?

There’s no sync, by design. Export on one device and import on another to move a workspace.

Does it call a server while I work?

No. Everything runs in the page: creating, editing, searching, filtering and dragging all happen locally.

Is it keyboard and screen-reader friendly?

Yes. Cards are focusable, can be picked up and moved with the keyboard, and moves are announced. Focus is always visible, and motion respects your reduced-motion setting.

Is this a commercial product?

No. FlowBoard is a portfolio project that demonstrates CRUD, state management and drag-and-drop in plain TypeScript, with no framework.
